Research — Active & Completed Projects

What we're building

Active

Quantum–Augmented Networks (QuANets)

A hybrid architecture that routes only privacy–sensitive traffic through quantum–secured channels, so quantum security can be deployed onto classical network infrastructure incrementally.

Active

Quantum Machine Learning for Network Security

Hybrid quantum–classical GANs and quantum–augmented defenses for critical infrastructure — adversarial network flow generation, microgrid security, and multimodal steganalysis.

Active

QUINTET — Quantum Internet Education & Training Synthesizer

An NSF–funded, AI–agent–assisted platform for teaching quantum networking through hands–on simulation and adaptive, learning–object–based courses. Built on Puja Anumula's foundational learning–objects platform and extended by Sahil Mangotra's AQUIRE thesis.

Completed

Quantum Anonymous Notification

A GHZ–state protocol that anonymously notifies a receiver of incoming quantum communication, resilient to dephasing noise. Published in Frontiers in Quantum Science and Technology, 2026.

Completed

Quantum Hilbert Transform

A quantum–domain formulation of the classical Hilbert transform, applied to quantum steganography — the first of its kind.
