Quantum Information
Lab

We apply the principles of quantum communication to strengthen network security, and study how quantum networks scale through a quantum–augmented networking approach — spanning key distribution, anonymous notification, and post–quantum defenses, evaluated against real channel noise.

Principal Investigator — Dr. Abhishek Parakh, Professor of Computer Science
